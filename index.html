Layerdle

        Demo mode: public-domain tunes synthesized in your browser. Run process_songs.py to add real songs.

        How to play

        1. Tap ▶ to hear the first layer: drums only.
        2. Guess the song, or skip. Each wrong guess or skip adds a layer: bass, then the other instruments, then vocals.
        3. The last try gives you a hint (year and the artist's first letter).
        4. Fewer layers = better score. There's a new daily song every midnight.

        Tap any unlocked layer to replay it. If you hear nothing on iPhone, check your volume.
